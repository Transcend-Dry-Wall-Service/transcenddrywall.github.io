Drywall Repair Services

I stay in my lane—small drywall work only—so you get quick scheduling and clean results without a huge crew.

Drywall Patch & Holes
Fix dents, doorknob holes, and random damage from everyday life. Cut out bad spots, patch, mud, sand, and blend into the wall.
Crack & Seam Repair
Repair cracks along seams, corners, and doors. Tape, mud, and smooth so the line disappears instead of coming back.
Water Damage Patches
Replace small sections ruined by leaks or plumbing repairs once the area is dry. Cut out, re-sheet, mud, and prep for paint.
Ceiling Repairs & Spots
Take care of small ceiling patches and stains so they’re ready for paint—no ladders or mess for you to deal with.
